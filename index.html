Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Kya aap pure Pakistan mein deliver karte hain?

Ji haan, hum tamam shehron aur gaon mein Cash on Delivery (COD) service provide karte hain.

2. Delivery charges kitne hain?

Standard delivery charges 200 PKR se 300 PKR ke darmiyan hote hain.

3. Order deliver hone mein kitna waqt lagta hai?

Aam tor par 3 se 5 working days mein parcel pohanch jata hai.

4. Kya parcel open kar ke check kar sakte hain?

Courier rules ke mutabiq pehle payment karni hoti hai, uske baad parcel open hota hai. Agar koi issue ho toh hamari 15-day return policy available hai.

5. Return ya Exchange ki kya policy hai?

Agar size ya product mein issue ho toh 15 din ke andar return/exchange karwa sakte hain ba-shart ke item unused ho aur tag laga ho.

6. Payment ke kon se methods available hain?

Cash on Delivery (COD), JazzCash, EasyPaisa, aur Bank Transfer.

7. Kya products 100% original hain?

Ji bilkul, hamare tamam electronics aur branded items genuine aur warranty ke sath aate hain.

8. Order tracking kaise hogi?

Dispatch hone ke baad tracking ID aapke WhatsApp number par bhej di jati hai.

9. Kya bulk ya wholesale order de sakte hain?

Ji haan, wholesale rates ke liye hamare contact form ke zariye raabta karein.

10. Order place hone ke baad address badal sakta hai?

Jab tak order "Pending" status mein hai tab tak WhatsApp par address change karwaya ja sakta hai.

11. Kya mobiles PTA approved hain?

Ji haan, hamare tamam phones 100% PTA approved hain.

12. Laptop ke sath kya accessories milti hain?

Original charger aur power cable har laptop ke sath di jati hai.

13. Shoes aur Garments ke sizes standard hain?

Ji haan, hum standard Pakistani/UK size chart follow karte hain.

14. Refund kitne din mein milta hai?

Return parcel receive hone ke 3 se 5 din ke andar JazzCash/EasyPaisa ya Bank account mein refund transfer ho jata hai.

15. Kya installment (qist) ki sahulat hai?

Filhal hum sirf full payment cash on delivery par deliver karte hain.

16. Out of stock products dobara kab aati hain?

Aam tor par 1 se 2 hafton ke andar inventory restock ho jati hai.

17. Kya koi hidden charges hain?

18. Agar wrong product receive ho jaye toh?
